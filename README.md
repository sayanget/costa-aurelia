An open-world coastal crime game built on three.js. Nico Rivas runs the streets of Costa
Aurelia — five districts from the Lowside Docks to the Hillside Villas — on foot and behind the
wheel of whatever he can steal, squeezed between Amber Court's enforcers and the Solstice Board's
fixers while the police try to catch up. Three story jobs, a wanted meter that spawns and loses a
chase, fists and a flare pistol, and a city that drives, walks and shoots back.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080

## Controls

- `WASD` — move / drive, `Shift` — sprint, `Space` — handbrake
- `F` or `E` — get in or out of the nearest vehicle
- Mouse — look around (click to lock the pointer); right click to aim
- Left click — punch or fire the held weapon
- `R` — reload, `1` — fists, `2` — flare pistol
- `Esc` — pause, save and change settings

Progress saves to `localStorage` automatically and from the pause menu; `Continue` on the title
screen picks it back up.
